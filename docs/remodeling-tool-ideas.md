# Remodeling Tool Ideas

Nov 20, 2026 · @Dylan

## How to use this sheet

A running list of the mundane puzzles small remodeling contractors solve by hand, and which ones could become fast field tools. Add ideas as they come up, mark what gets built, and note what field testing turns up.

**Built so far:**

- Niche Rough-In Planner (tile layout, niche, framing, drawing sheets)
- Bathroom Site Survey (field checklist and report)
- Bathroom Floor Plan (existing, framing and design views with clearance checks)
- Vanity Wall Solver (sink, toilet, mirror, lights, outlet, storage cabinet, with setup questions)

## Layout puzzles

Same family as the niche: where to start so the cuts come out clean.

- **Floor tile layout.** Where to start so there are no slivers at the door or tub; centering on the room or the toilet. Pairs with the wall tile planner and the floor plan.
- **Shower pan and curb.** Slope from the walls to the drain, mortar bed thickness at the walls, curb height and width. Linear drains change the math.
- **Grab bar blocking.** ADA or aging-in-place heights and lengths at the toilet, tub and shower, and the blocking to frame before drywall.
- **Wainscot or beadboard.** Panel and rail spacing so joints land evenly around windows, outlets and corners.
- **Backsplash tile.** Centering on the range or sink, clean cuts at outlets and cabinet ends.

## Fit puzzles

- **Stairs.** Riser and tread counts from the total rise, headroom, stringer layout. A classic that nobody enjoys redoing on site.
- **Door and window rough openings.** RO from the unit size, header sizing for the span and load, jamb extensions for wall thickness.
- **Kitchen cabinet run.** Fitting standard boxes into a wall length, with fillers, appliance openings and corner clearances. The vanity solver on a bigger scale.
- **Will it fit through the house.** Tub, vanity or appliance against door widths, hallway turns and stairs. Partly covered by the site survey already.

## Material puzzles

- **Takeoffs with real waste.** Tile by pattern (herringbone wastes more), drywall sheets by wall size, trim by piece length so joints land on studs.
- **Trim cut lists.** Casing, baseboard and crown with miter lengths, scarf joint locations, and the fewest pieces from stock lengths.
- **Thinset, grout and membrane quantities** from tile size and joint width.

## Check puzzles

- **Kitchen code clearances**, like the bathroom checks: work aisles, landing space beside appliances, outlet spacing.
- **Deck or landing stair code checks:** guard height, baluster spacing, stair geometry.

## Top picks and next steps

- **Floor tile layout** and **shower pan slope** are the best next builds. They are the same kind of puzzle as the niche, contractors buying the bathroom service would want them, and they plug into the floor plan.
- **Stairs** is the strongest stand-alone tool if one outside bathrooms is ever wanted.
