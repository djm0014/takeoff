# Pre-Bid Bathroom Survey

Nov 20, 2026 · @Dylan · Draft

## What it is

I measure and document the bathroom before you bid, so you price the job from a complete report instead of a second site visit. You get the measurements, existing conditions and the problems that usually turn into change orders, in a standard format, within 24 hours of the visit. You pay for the survey whether or not you win the job.

## Tier 1: Pre-bid survey

Flat fee of \[price\] per bathroom, report within 24 hours. Add a second bathroom for \[price\] or an attic or crawlspace check for \[price\].

| Area | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Measurements | Wall, floor and ceiling dimensions; floor area, net wall areas, room volume; level, plumb and square readings |
| Fixtures | Tub or shower size and drain side, toilet rough-in, vanity size, valve brand and type, shutoff condition |
| Plumbing | Supply and drain pipe material, water heater size and age |
| Electrical and fan | GFCI protection, circuit sizes, light and switch locations; whether the fan vents outside; fan size (CFM) for the room |
| Structure | Joist size, span and direction; subfloor thickness; moisture readings and photos of any damage |
| Hazards | Flags for lead paint and asbestos based on house age and materials, with testing recommended where needed |
| Logistics | Path from street to bathroom (door widths, stairs, turns), parking, dumpster spot, whether it is the only bathroom |
| Photos | Every wall, fixture, under the sink, and the ceiling or floor above and below |

## Tier 2: Construction package

Quoted per job after you win the bid, starting at \[price\]. Built from the survey, so nothing is measured twice.

- Tile layout with no slivers, first course and ledger heights, cut list
- Niche rough-in: framing sizes, sill and head heights, shelf and sill details
- Framing elevation and blocking, using the existing studs
- Drawing sheets: elevations, niche section, notes, ready to print at 11 × 17
- Tub fit check, with furring flagged where the tile won't reach the tub lip
- Material takeoff with waste worked out from the layout

## How it works

1. You book the survey and tell me the homeowner and address.
2. I schedule with the homeowner and visit as your representative, in about \[60–90\] minutes on site.
3. You get the report and photos within 24 hours.
4. You bid. If you win, I quote the construction package.

## Terms

- **Your data:** the report belongs to the contractor who paid for it. If two of my clients bid the same house, each gets a separate visit and report.
- **Verification:** the report is a basis for bidding. Verify dimensions and conditions before ordering materials or building.
- **Not an inspection:** this is a measurement and conditions survey, not a licensed home inspection or hazard test.
